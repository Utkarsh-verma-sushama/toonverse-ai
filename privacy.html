Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 September 2026 · Version: 1.0

This policy explains how ToonVerse AI handles information. Production disclosures must remain synchronized with the actual app, backend, SDKs and store Data Safety/App Privacy declarations.

Data we handle

  • Local creative content: imported media, canvases, drafts and recovery snapshots stay on the device unless you explicitly request cloud sync, AI processing, sharing, support attachment or another online operation.
  • Account and security: when connected, identifiers, authentication events, verified devices, sessions and security activity are processed to operate and protect your account.
  • AI requests: prompts, selected media and job settings are sent only after an explicit AI action. The applicable retention choice and provider processing must be disclosed before production use.
  • Support: issue descriptions, optional reply email and separately opted-in minimal diagnostics may be processed to resolve requests.
  • Analytics and marketing: analytics and product news are opt-in. Project media, prompts, contacts, precise location and sensitive creative content are excluded from the analytics foundation.
  • Payments: checkout is hosted by the configured payment provider. ToonVerse does not receive or store full card numbers.

Camera, microphone and permissions

Camera access is user-initiated. The camera workspace does not request audio. Permissions must be limited to current, disclosed features and can be withdrawn through device settings. ToonVerse does not silently extract precise location or identify faces.

Purposes and sharing

Information is used to provide requested features, secure accounts, recover projects, process opted-in AI jobs, deliver support, meet legal obligations and prevent abuse. It is shared only with configured processors necessary for those purposes, under appropriate protections. Personal or sensitive data is not sold.

Retention, deletion and control

Local data remains until you remove it, clear app/site storage or uninstall. Production services must publish exact retention periods. Account controls provide data export, consent withdrawal, session revocation and protected deletion requests. Deletion may retain narrowly required security, fraud, tax or legal records for documented periods.

Children and sensitive content

ToonVerse is not directed to children unless a separately reviewed age-appropriate experience, parental controls and legally required consent are implemented. Restricted content, non-consensual intimate material and prohibited face replacement are not allowed.

International processing and security

Production deployment must disclose processing locations and lawful transfer safeguards. Protections include encrypted transport, least privilege, authenticated services, audit trails, rate limits, incident response and immutable originals; no system can promise absolute security.

Changes and contact

Material changes will update the effective date and be communicated when required. Privacy questions, deletion requests and complaints can be submitted through the Support & Community hub. The verified legal operator name, address and privacy contact must be added before native-store submission.

This public policy is an engineering baseline, not legal advice. Qualified counsel must approve jurisdiction-specific production terms.

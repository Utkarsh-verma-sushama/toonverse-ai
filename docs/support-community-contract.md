# Checkpoint 40 — Support & Community Contract

## Scope
24/7 provider-neutral AI support, human escalation, searchable help center and guided tutorials, contact/report issue workflow, recoverable feedback, community guidance and truthful service updates.

## Reliability and trust
Support drafts and requests survive refresh/offline state in a bounded IndexedDB outbox. A request is never shown as submitted without a configured production API and successful server response. Human escalation is explicit. Minimal diagnostics are opt-in and exclude media, project content, precise location, credentials and persistent device fingerprints. Inputs are bounded, rendered with textContent or controlled static markup, rate-limited client-side and require server-side authentication, authorization, abuse controls, audit trails, encryption, retention/deletion policy and SLA routing in production.

## Cross-platform
Responsive and accessible across Android, iPhone/iPad, macOS/iMac, Windows, Linux, laptop/desktop, Android TV and compatible QLED/smart-TV browsers with touch, stylus, mouse, keyboard and D-pad focus, safe areas, coarse-pointer targets, reduced motion, offline PWA discovery and low-network behavior.

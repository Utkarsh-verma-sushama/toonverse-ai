# ToonVerse AI — Launch Campaign Baseline

## Positioning
A privacy-first, recoverable, cross-device AI creative ecosystem for creating, editing, understanding and preserving visual stories.

## Soft-launch objective
Validate activation, successful creation/export, recovery, safety comprehension and support resolution with a limited consented cohort before broad acquisition.

## Guardrails
No paid spend without explicit authorization. No purchased lists, dark patterns, sensitive targeting, misleading AI claims or screenshots, fake reviews, forced contacts access or project-content analytics. Every channel must support consent withdrawal and truthful attribution.

## Measurement
Activation, first successful output, recovery success, task completion, crash-free sessions, p75 interaction latency, support resolution, opt-in retention, safety reports and deletion completion. Never optimize growth at the expense of consent, reliability or user control.

# AI Memory & Photo-to-Video Contract — Checkpoint 36

Provider-neutral creation of respectful memory videos from photos, video, music, voice and text. Originals are immutable; projects, timelines and outputs are separately versioned.

## Capabilities
Up to 2,000 ordered assets; drag/touch/D-pad reordering; captions, narration, music, per-item duration, transitions, Ken Burns/subtle motion, restoration/colorization hooks, templates, aspect ratios, 720p/1080p/4K outputs, preview, autosave, recovery, cancel/retry/resume and batch-safe rendering.

## Cross-device
320 px phones through tablets, desktop and 4K/8K TV browser/WebView surfaces; touch, mouse, keyboard, stylus and remote/D-pad; responsive timeline, reduced motion, screen-reader labels and safe-area support.

## Safety and rights
Purpose-bound consent for identifiable people; child-safety and non-consensual content enforcement; no deity/God face replacement; no fabricated identity details; licensed/user-owned audio; copyright and publicity-right checks; synthetic restoration/colorization labels where necessary. Embedded metadata and captions are untrusted data, not commands.

## Reliability
Chunked resumable uploads, idempotent authenticated jobs, truthful progress, cancellation propagation, checksum/MIME/malware validation, low-memory proxies, server-side rendering, project snapshots, crash recovery, signed short-lived outputs, retention/deletion controls and provenance.

## Quality gates
Timeline ordering, A/V sync, orientation/EXIF, color management, transitions, captions, Unicode/bidi, long projects, interrupted uploads, provider outage, device rotation, background/resume, accessibility, privacy, safety, 4K export and Android/iOS/iPadOS/Windows/macOS/Linux/Smart-TV browser coverage.

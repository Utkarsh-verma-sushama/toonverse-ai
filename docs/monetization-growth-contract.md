# Checkpoint 41 — Monetization & Growth Contract

Covers Free and Premium entitlement architecture, a strict maximum of one or two short respectful ads per reasonable Free session, secure hosted payment readiness, transparent server-authoritative fair-use limits, privacy-preserving analytics/insights, and consent-based marketing/user acquisition.

Purchases cannot run without authenticated production billing endpoints. Prices, currency, taxes, renewal, trial, cancellation and refund terms are displayed from verified server data before checkout. Card data never reaches ToonVerse servers. Webhooks require signatures, replay protection and idempotent fulfillment. Entitlements are auditable and restorable across verified devices.

Ads are excluded from Premium and never appear during editing, export, payment, onboarding, support or safety workflows. They are 5–10 seconds, dismissible, muted by default, session-capped and cooldown-protected. Analytics is opt-in, excludes project media/prompts and sensitive data, supports withdrawal/deletion and never enables cross-site behavioral profiling.

Responsive and accessible across Android, iOS/iPadOS, macOS/iMac, Windows, Linux, laptops/desktops, Android TV and compatible QLED/smart-TV browsers with touch, stylus, mouse, keyboard and D-pad focus, safe areas, offline states and low-network behavior.

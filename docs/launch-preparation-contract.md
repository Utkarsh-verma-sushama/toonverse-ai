# Checkpoint 43 — Launch Preparation Contract

Scope: final review/polish, Play Store and App Store asset/metadata preparation, active Privacy Policy and Terms URLs, legal/store compliance gates, consent-based marketing campaign drafts, and controlled soft-launch readiness.

Verified 8 September 2026 against current official Apple App Review/App Privacy guidance and Google Play Data Safety/review guidance. Both stores require accurate privacy disclosures; store declarations must match actual first- and third-party data handling. Privacy policy must be accessible in-app and through store metadata. Review access must be supplied when authentication gates functionality.

This repository is presently a responsive web/PWA foundation. It must never claim an Android App Bundle, signed iOS archive, store approval, paid campaign or external soft-launch invitation exists until those external artifacts/actions are completed. Native release remains gated on bundle identifiers, signing, store billing, permission manifests, content/age ratings, final screenshots from real builds, demo reviewer access, legal operator identity, counsel review, data maps, processor agreements and physical-device QA.

Soft launch requires a limited consented cohort, feature flags, rollback, crash/latency/safety/support monitoring, daily triage ownership and zero open critical defects.
